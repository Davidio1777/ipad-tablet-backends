# Privacy Policy — iPad Linux Tablet

Effective date: August 9, 2026

iPad Linux Tablet turns an iPad and Apple Pencil into a display and pen tablet for a computer owned
and operated by the user on the same local network or through a paired USB connection.

## Data handled by the app

The app processes the backend address and token entered by the user, Apple Pencil coordinates,
pressure, tilt and button events, video received from the user's computer, StoreKit purchase
entitlements, rewarded-ad progress, and the expiration time of a 24-hour rewarded unlock.

This information is used only on the device or sent directly between the iPad and the backend chosen
by the user. LAN datagrams are encrypted and authenticated. The developer does not operate a relay,
analytics relay or account system and does not receive or store this computer/tablet data.

## Rewarded advertising

Users without Lifetime access may voluntarily complete two Google AdMob rewarded ads to unlock the
app for 24 hours. The app requests non-personalized ads and uses Google's User Messaging Platform to
present privacy choices where required. Google Mobile Ads may process IP-derived coarse location,
device identifiers, ad and product interactions, crash information, diagnostics and performance data
to deliver, secure and measure advertising. This data is processed by Google under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and is not combined by the developer
with Pencil samples, video, backend addresses or LAN tokens.

The **Ad Privacy Choices** control in the app reopens Google's privacy options when they are required.
The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and does not request
personalized ads.

## Purchases

Lifetime purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may process purchase and account
information under Apple's own privacy policy. The app receives only the verified entitlement needed
to unlock the feature.

## Data collection and tracking

The developer does not operate analytics or create user profiles. Google processes the limited data
described above for rewarded advertising and related measurement. The app does not request tracking
permission or deliberately track users across apps and websites.

## Retention and deletion

Connection settings remain in app memory for the current run. Rewarded-ad progress, the 24-hour
unlock expiration, and consent state are stored locally on the iPad. Removing the app deletes local
app data. Purchase records remain with Apple and can be restored through the App Store. Google may
retain advertising data according to its own privacy and retention policies.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose productivity tool and does not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes and contact

Material policy changes will be published in this file with a new effective date. Questions can be
