# Privacy Policy — iPad Linux Tablet

Effective date: August 9, 2026

iPad Linux Tablet turns an iPad and Apple Pencil into a display and pen tablet for a computer owned
and operated by the user on the same local network or through a paired USB connection.

## Data handled by the app

The app processes the backend address and token entered by the user, Apple Pencil coordinates,
pressure, tilt and button events, video received from the user's computer, StoreKit purchase
entitlements, and the amount of free usage remaining for the current local day.

This information is used only on the device or sent directly between the iPad and the backend chosen
by the user. LAN datagrams are encrypted and authenticated. The developer does not operate a relay,
analytics service, advertising service or account system and does not receive or store this data.

## Purchases

Lifetime purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may process purchase and account
information under Apple's own privacy policy. The app receives only the verified entitlement needed
to unlock the feature.

## Data collection and tracking

The app does not collect personal data, use third-party analytics, show advertising, create tracking
profiles, or track users across apps and websites.

## Retention and deletion

Connection settings remain in app memory for the current run. Daily free-usage counters are stored in
iPadOS UserDefaults. Removing the app deletes local app data. Purchase records remain with Apple and
can be restored through the App Store.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose productivity tool and does not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes and contact

Material policy changes will be published in this file with a new effective date. Questions can be
opened through the public project's [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Davidio1777/ipad-tablet-backends/issues).
